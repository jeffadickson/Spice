# Bounded MOS LEVEL=1

The qualified profile uses the Shichman-Hodges channel with analytic four-terminal derivatives. Model terms: LEVEL=1 (default), VTO, KP, GAMMA, PHI, LAMBDA, IS, CBD, CBS, PB, MJ, FC, CGSO, CGDO, CGBO, TNOM=27, TOX, RD and RS. Positive L and W resolve from instance overrides, model geometry, or the 100 µm defaults. The binder refuses additional terms. Effective beta is KP*W/L. Nonzero GAMMA requires nonpositive body bias relative to the effective source, including reversed operation.

Defaults follow the pinned SpiceSharp 3.2.3 source: VTO=0, KP=2e-5 A/V², GAMMA=0, PHI=.6 V, LAMBDA=0, IS=1e-14 A, CBD/CBS=0 F, PB=.8 V, MJ=.5, FC=.5, overlap factors=0. The source-reference commit is `34484fa6c66d636f6983626a0a770f75c0484553`. The MIT license and attribution are retained under `third-party`.

The native `mos1_channel` law contributes channel conduction and optional intrinsic Meyer displacement. The `i_channel(id)` observable reports conduction. Generated M cards compose body junctions with the qualified nonlinear depletion Q and overlap capacitors CGSO*W, CGDO*W, CGBO*L. An outer zero-volt drain sense supplies signed total `i(M)` including displacement. Accepted-step BE history and rollback belong to the existing primitives; no private mutable MOS charge state is introduced.

Positive TOX derives intrinsic oxide capacitance from silicon-dioxide permittivity and the resolved W × L area. When KP is omitted, the binder derives it using the ngspice default mobility of 600 cm²/V/s. Zero or omitted TOX leaves body and overlap storage active and keeps the 20 µA/V² KP default. The bounded profile uses resolved dimensions directly and currently refuses LD, UO, area and sidewall parameters.
