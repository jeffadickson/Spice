## Third Reference Slice — Reproducible Variation Campaigns

Iterations 101–150 add bounded immutable scalar variations and evidence improvements. They do not qualify distributed execution, hostile-input sandboxing, nonlinear models, or a production StressForge integration.

**REF3-001 — Immutable scalar variation**

Variations clone and validate the public circuit representation instead of mutating compiled plans.

**REF3-002 — Bounded deterministic variation products**

Scalar sweeps and grids preserve declared dimension/value ordering, validate bounded inputs, and assign stable point identities.

**REF3-003 — Point-isolated variation execution**

Each completed point has a success/failure result. Cancellation is checked between points.

**REF3-004 — Reproducible seeded scalar plans**

Uniform and positive log-uniform plans record seed, range, and algorithm identifier. The PRNG is for reproducibility and is not cryptographic or a statistical qualification.

**REF3-005 — Programmatic variation result projection**

The API reports selected identity/unit, per-point status, final signal and measurement rows, and assertion counts without converting missing data into passing results.
